---
Title: Can love at first sight happen?
Chapter: conclusion
Subject: 亲密关系
---
Some individuals believe in love at first sight, because they experience a phenomenon of feeling euphoria and desiring to explore a further relationship. Whereas, it boils down to an influx of hormones which triggers you to seek closeness and intimacy.

The long-lasting relationship depends on dozens of factors, not limited to the openness of love and intimacy styles, the attachment style and the communication style. The development of this kind of relationship does not automatically last forever,it needs hard work, good habits of communication and commitment to each other. You should establish a history of trust and commitment, and you will feel safe with one another emotionally, spiritually, mentally and physically. Though it would not be without hills and valleys, you will feel like working as a team towards a bright future.

In conclusion, the initial feelings that individuals describe during love at first sight often fade with time. However, true attachment — engaged in a healthy relationship — will grow over time.
